# Oversized Hoodie: Internal Specifications

**Internal use only.** Proposed finished-garment targets for sampling and development.

- **Size range:** S to XXXL.
- **Fit:** Oversized.
- **Base size:** XL.
- **Units:** Centimeters (cm). Measure the garment flat without stretching.
- **Interchangeable design:** Every size uses the same finished 30 cm wide x 40 cm high panel, centered on the back.
- **Confirmed XL placement:** 20 cm vertically from the center-back hood/neck attachment seam to the top edge of the finished panel.

## 3. Rules used for this draft

- Chest width, body length, and shoulder width: **+3 cm per size**.
- Sleeve length: **+1 cm per size**.
- Hood height: **40 cm estimated for XL; +0.5 cm per size**.
- Panel width and height: **30 x 40 cm for every size**.
- Neckline-to-panel-top position: **20 cm for XL; proposed +3 cm per size**.

The proposed panel-position step follows the body-length step. Its purpose is to preserve the XL panel's clearance above the bottom rib as sizes change, assuming the usable center-back length also changes by 3 cm per size.

**Different reference points:** Body length starts at the high shoulder point beside the neckline. Panel placement starts at the center-back neckline. Do not subtract one from the other to claim an exact hem clearance.

## 4. How to measure

- **Chest width:** Straight across the front, just below the armholes, with the garment flat.
- **Body length:** From the highest shoulder point beside the neckline straight down to the bottom hem, including the ribbed band and excluding the hood.
- **Shoulder width:** Straight across the back between the tops of the sleeve attachment seams.
- **Sleeve length:** From the sleeve attachment seam along the upper outside edge to the end of the ribbed cuff.
- **Panel position:** Lift the hood out of the way. Start at the center-back point of the hood/neck attachment seam and measure vertically to the finished panel's top edge.
- **Panel size:** Finished width 30 cm; finished height 40 cm. Raw cut sizes and any extra attachment border must be specified separately by the maker.
- **Hood height:** Fold the hood flat along its center seam so one side faces up. Measure its straight overall height between the attachment/base level and the highest crown point. This is a straight height, not distance along a curved seam.

## 5. Checks for the maker

### Back-panel clearance

Measure **U**, the usable center-back distance from the hood/neck attachment seam to the top edge of the bottom rib, on each sample.

To preserve the XL sample's actual clearance above the rib, use:

**Top offset for a size = 20 + (U for that size - U for XL).**

The panel's bottom clearance above the rib is **U - top offset - 40**. Measure U before assigning a numeric clearance. Confirm that the panel and its attachment border clear the rib, particularly on S and XXXL.

### Interchangeability and visibility

- Keep the same finished panel size, orientation, fastener type, and attachment positions on every hoodie size.
- Record attachment positions relative to the finished panel and reuse that layout across sizes. Fastener layout and allowances are not supplied by the current measurements.
- Check the hoodie with the hood lowered naturally. The amount the hood covers the back panel cannot be calculated from hood height alone. Review this especially on the smaller sizes, where the panel starts higher.
- Make an XL hood sample using the estimated 40 cm height, check its fit, then validate the other hood sizes. Hood depth, neckline matching, and pattern construction remain maker specifications.

## 6. Basis for the hood estimate

Create Fashion Brand's oversized hoodie technical pack, page 2, lists a 39.5 cm hood height for its grouped XL/XXL size. This supports using approximately 40 cm as an initial XL height reference. The 0.5 cm per-size step and the measurement method defined here are proposed for this project; they are not a universal size standard or proof of fit.
