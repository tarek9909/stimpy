# Stimpy — Brand Identity

**One hoodie. Multiple identities.**

---

## 1. The Brand in One Line

Stimpy is a premium hoodie with an interchangeable back panel called a **Stimpie**. One hoodie, endless looks. The identity is built to reflect that idea: a strong, fixed core with room for change.

**Personality:** confident, clever, playful, clean.
**Language:** English only.

---

## 2. The Logo

### The idea: the split S

The icon is a letter **S** split down the middle:

- **The left half is solid.** It represents the hoodie: the stable base that stays the same.
- **The right half is an outline, dropped slightly lower.** It represents the Stimpie: the side of you that changes.

Together they say what Stimpy is about: two sides, one identity.

### Logo elements

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **Icon** | The split S on its own. |
| **Wordmark** | "Stimpy" in a bold geometric sans-serif. |
| **Full logo** | Icon on the left, wordmark on the right, vertically centered. |

### The three official versions

| Version | Background | Solid half | Outline half | Wordmark |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Main logo** | Coral | Black | White | Black |
| **On black** | Black | White | Coral | White |
| **On white** | White | Black | Coral | Black |

### When to use each version

**Main logo (coral background)**
The brand's signature moment. Use it when the goal is for people to instantly recognize Stimpy.
- Instagram profile picture (icon only)
- Launch announcements, drop reveals, and key posts
- Packaging, stickers, and the first or last frame of videos
- Anywhere the brand should feel bold and energetic

**On black**
The premium, night-time version. Use it when the mood is stronger and more exclusive.
- Dark product photography and black hoodie shoots
- Teasers, countdowns, and "coming soon" content
- Labels and embroidery on black hoodies
- Evening or limited-edition drop content

**On white**
The clean, everyday version. Use it when clarity matters most.
- Website pages and order forms
- Documents, invoices, and hang tags
- Light product photography and white or cream hoodies
- Any layout with a lot of text or information

### When to use the icon alone vs. the full logo

- **Icon alone:** small or square spaces where the name would be too small to read, such as the Instagram profile picture, favicon, woven labels, zipper pulls, stickers, and the small signature mark on each Stimpie.
- **Full logo:** anywhere there is enough space for the name to be clearly readable, such as the website header, packaging, posters, and the end frame of videos.

### Logo rules

- Always keep clear space around the logo, at least the width of the solid half of the S.
- Never change the logo's colors outside the three official versions.
- Never use a secondary color for the logo.
- Never stretch, rotate, add shadows, or add effects to the logo.
- Never place the logo on busy photos without a solid background behind it.
- The logo must stay readable at small sizes; for anything very small (such as embroidery under 2 cm), use the icon alone.

## 3. Colors

### Primary colors

These three colors are the brand. They should make up the majority of everything Stimpy publishes.

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coral** | `#FF7A59` | 255, 122, 89 | Signature color. The color people remember as Stimpy. |
| **Black** | `#111111` | 17, 17, 17 | Text, dark backgrounds, premium moments. |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Clean backgrounds, text on dark, website. |

### Secondary colors

These support the brand as highlights and drop colors. They never replace the primary colors and are never used for the logo.

| Name | HEX | RGB | Best used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Electric blue** | `#2E63FF` | 46, 99, 255 | Calls to action: "Order now," countdowns, announcements. |
| **Butter** | `#FFE45C` | 255, 228, 92 | Drop color. Warm, sunny campaigns. |
| **Sky** | `#5CC8FF` | 92, 200, 255 | Drop color. Fresh, light campaigns. |
| **Lilac** | `#C9B6FF` | 201, 182, 255 | Drop color. Creative, softer campaigns. |
| **Cream** | `#FFF4E6` | 255, 244, 230 | Warm alternative to white: packaging paper, hang tags, backgrounds. |

### How to use the colors

- **Primary colors lead every design.** Coral, black, and white should be the majority of any post, page, or package.
- **Secondary colors are highlights.** Use no more than one or two in the same design.
- **Each drop can own a secondary color.** For example, a drop's teasers, stories, and countdown can use Butter, while the next drop uses Sky. The core brand stays the same while the feed stays fresh.
- **Electric blue is for action.** Keep it for buttons and moments that need attention, so it keeps its impact.

### Text contrast

| Background | Use text in |
|---|---|
| Coral | Black |
| Black | White |
| White | Black |
| Electric blue | White |
| Butter, Sky, Lilac, Cream | Black |

## 4. Typography

| Use | Font | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark and headlines | Poppins | Bold |
| Subheadings | Poppins | Medium |
| Body text and captions | Poppins | Regular |

Poppins is free on Google Fonts. Keep headlines short and punchy, always in English.

---

## 5. On the Product

- **Front of the hoodie:** a small embroidered icon or wordmark on the chest, tone-on-tone or in a primary color. Keep it minimal so the Stimpie on the back remains the statement.
  - Black hoodie: white or coral embroidery.
  - White or cream hoodie: black embroidery.
- **Signature details:** a coral touch on the inside of the hood, the zipper pull, or the woven label.
- **Stimpies:** each panel carries the small split S icon in a corner, so every design is signed.
- **Packaging:** coral or black boxes with the full logo, cream tissue paper, and a Stimpy sticker inside.

---

## 6. On Instagram

- Most posts sit on coral, black, white, or cream backgrounds, with the hoodie and Stimpie as the hero.
- Use the main logo (icon only) as the profile picture.
- During a drop period, that drop's secondary color takes over teasers, stories, and countdowns.
- Start or end every video with the logo and the signature zip sound.
