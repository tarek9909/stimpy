# 04 · The Hidden Back: shoot ideas

**Brief:** Someone walks away from the camera with the back blurred, covered or out of frame. Caption: "The back changes. Soon."

Each idea below is a filmable concept for the real footage. Each folder has:
- an animatic, to watch the timing, framing and sound;
- the same animatic with the shoot notes on screen, for whoever films;
- a shoot brief (`README.md`) with the kit, setup and a timed shot list.

| Idea | Length | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| [The Shy Back](a_the_shy_back/) | 10.4 s | The camera circles. They keep turning to face it. They finally turn… cut. |
| [Always Blocked](b_always_blocked/) | 10 s | Five streets, and each time something is perfectly in the way of the back. |
| [Colour Leak Walk](c_colour_leak_walk/) | 10.6 s | A silhouette walks into a light that changes colour on every footstep. |
| [Out of Frame](d_out_of_frame/) | 10 s | A handheld follow that is clearly not allowed to show the back. |
| [Censored](e_censored/) | 10.4 s | A sunny walk away with the back pixelated, glitching for two frames at a time. |

Every one ends on a ready-made card from [`../00_end_cards`](../00_end_cards/), which carries the signature zip sound.

> The old renders still in this folder (stimpy_hidden.mp4/.wav, hidden_sheet.png) are the first motion version you didn't like. It has been replaced by the ideas above. Delete it whenever you like.
