# 04 · D · Out of Frame

**Shoot brief for the real-footage version of the idea. 9:16, 10 s.**

A handheld follow from behind where the camera is clearly not allowed to show the back. Shoes only: a tilt up, snapped back down. The hood only: a tilt down, jerked back up. A sleeve, then a whip pan across the back so fast it's a smear. Then only their shadow on the pavement. A final tilt up… cut.

## Files
- `stimpy_outofframe_clean.mp4`: the animatic. It shows the timing, framing and sound to aim for.
- `stimpy_outofframe_notes.mp4`: the same film with the shoot notes on screen (shot number, what to do). Give this one to whoever is filming.
- `outofframe_clean_sheet.png`: contact sheet. `.wav`: the animatic soundtrack.

## Kit
- A phone, handheld
- Sun low in the sky (morning or late afternoon) for the shadow shot

## Setup
Follow close behind, framed tight. The operator acts as if the back is off-limits.

## Shot list (timed to the animatic)
| Time | Shot | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 s | **Shoes only** | Framed tight on the shoes. Tilt up slowly, stop at the hem and snap straight back down. |
| 1.9 s | **Hood only** | Cut. Framed on the hood. Start to tilt down, then jerk back up. |
| 3.6 s | **A sleeve** | Cut. Framed on one sleeve swinging as they walk. |
| 4.5 s | **Whip pan** | Whip-pan hard across the back to the other sleeve. The motion blur hides everything. |
| 5.1 s | **Only the shadow** | Cut. Point down at the pavement: shoes at the top, their shadow stretching toward you (sun low in front of them). |
| 6.8 s | **The final tilt…** | Back on the shoes. Start tilting up… |
| 7.5 s | **…cut** | Add `00_end_cards/stimpy_endcards_soon.mp4` before anything shows. |

## Sound
A sneaky, tiptoeing bass line. Add a swish and a bloop on every "nope", and a big whoosh on the whip.

## Edit
Shoot at 60 fps with a fast shutter, except for the whip pan, where you want the blur.

## Caption
"Our camera person isn't allowed to show it yet."
