# 04 · B · Always Blocked

**Shoot brief for the real-footage version of the idea. 9:16, 10 s.**

A running gag. We follow someone from behind through five different streets, and every time something is exactly in the way of the back: a box, a pole, a hand on the lens, a jacket, a "Not yet." pop-up. Then the zip itself closes over everything.

## Files
- `stimpy_blocked_clean.mp4`: the animatic. It shows the timing, framing and sound to aim for.
- `stimpy_blocked_notes.mp4`: the same film with the shoot notes on screen (shot number, what to do). Give this one to whoever is filming.
- `blocked_clean_sheet.png`: contact sheet. `.wav`: the animatic soundtrack.

## Kit
- A phone (handheld or gimbal)
- A friend with a big cardboard box
- A coral or bright jacket
- Five spots around the city or campus

## Setup
Follow at about 2 m behind the subject, at chest height, in a different street for each clip. Each clip only needs 1.5 s.

## Shot list (timed to the animatic)
| Time | Shot | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 s | **The box** | A friend crosses in front carrying a big box, perfectly in the way of the back. |
| 1.5 s | **The pole** | Cut. Walk past a pole or tree that covers the back the whole time. Keep the camera low and the pole close to the lens so it is blurred. |
| 3.0 s | **Hand on the lens** | Cut. The operator's free hand comes up over the lens, right on the back. |
| 4.5 s | **The jacket** | Cut. They have a jacket slung over the shoulder, hanging down the back. |
| 6.0 s | **Not yet.** | Cut. A "Not yet." pop-up (made in the edit) lands on the back. |
| 7.5 s | **End card** | Add `00_end_cards/stimpy_endcards_soon.mp4`. |

## Sound
An upbeat track at 120 bpm. Cut on the beat (every 3 beats), and add a small "nope" bloop on each cut.

## Edit
Hard cuts on the beat. Make each block feel deliberate, and get the timing right.

## Caption
"We tried to film the back. Five times. The back changes. Soon."
