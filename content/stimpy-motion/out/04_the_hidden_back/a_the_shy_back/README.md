# 04 · A · The Shy Back

**Shoot brief for the real-footage version of the idea. 9:16, 10.4 s.**

The camera circles someone to see their back, and they keep turning to face it: a beat late, then a fake-out, then a full spin that gets out of hand. They stop, dizzy. Finally they start to turn away… and it cuts to "The back changes. Soon." It shows the brand's playful, clever side.

## Files
- `stimpy_shyback_clean.mp4`: the animatic. It shows the timing, framing and sound to aim for.
- `stimpy_shyback_notes.mp4`: the same film with the shoot notes on screen (shot number, what to do). Give this one to whoever is filming.
- `shyback_clean_sheet.png`: contact sheet. `.wav`: the animatic soundtrack.

## Kit
- A phone, ideally on a gimbal
- A friend to film
- A plain wall or room. A few coloured boards around the room make the orbit read better.

## Setup
Film from 1.5 m away at chest height. The subject wears the hoodie with the hood up and stands on one spot (a tape mark on the floor).

## Shot list (timed to the animatic)
| Time | Shot | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 s | **Front, mid shot** | The subject faces the lens. |
| 1.2 s | **Camera starts circling** | The operator walks a slow circle. The subject turns a beat late, then snaps back to face the lens. |
| 1.95 s | **Fake-out** | The operator suddenly reverses direction. The subject catches it. |
| 3.0 s | **Faster, faster** | The operator jogs the circle and the subject spins to keep up. Shoot at 60 fps. |
| 6.2 s | **Stop. Dizzy.** | The camera stops dead. The subject wobbles and laughs. |
| 7.0 s | **The slow turn…** | The subject finally starts to turn their back to the lens… |
| 7.8 s | **…cut** | Cut BEFORE the back shows. Add `00_end_cards/stimpy_endcards_soon.mp4`. |

## Sound
A playful, bouncy track. Add a "boing" on each snap back, whooshes in the spin, and a record-stop when it stops.

## Edit
Keep it in one take if you can. The comedy is in the timing.

## Caption
"Why won't it turn around? 🙈 The back changes. Soon."
