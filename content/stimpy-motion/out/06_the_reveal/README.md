# 06 · The Reveal

**Brief (#6):** The full swap. Hands unzip one Stimpie and zip on another in a few seconds, then the person turns around. It ends with the launch date. This is the most important video of the campaign.

**Format:** 14.5 s, 9:16, 1080×1920, 60 fps, 120 bpm. Posted in week 4, before the Split Screen and the Countdown.

## The film

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0.5–1.25 s | Close on the back of a black hoodie wearing FLAME ("LOUD") on coral. A hand in a black sleeve reaches for the coral pull. |
| 1.35–2.55 s | It unzips the Stimpie all the way round, one click per tooth. |
| 2.6–3.15 s | It peels the Stimpie off and throws it out of frame. The bare back shows the clean dock seam. |
| 2.95–4.75 s | A second hand brings in WAVE. It lands with a squash and gets zipped on all the way round. |

| 5.0–7.5 s | The camera pulls back to the whole figure while the back keeps changing on the beat (night → bloom → sun → tag), and the world re-colours each time. "One hoodie. / Multiple identities." |
| 8.0–9.0 s | The person turns around to face you: the faceless hood (it could be anyone) with coral lining and the split S on the chest. |
| 9.5 s | Signature zip wipe → the launch date on coral, with the logo, "LAUNCH DAY", the date and an electric-blue "Set a reminder". |
