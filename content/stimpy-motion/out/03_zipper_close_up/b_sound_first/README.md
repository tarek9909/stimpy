# 03 · B · Sound First

**Shoot brief for the real-footage version of the idea. 9:16, 9 s.**

Start on a black screen with only the zip sound, which stops the scroll. Then hard-cut to the macro on the final teeth: *clack*. It opens again slowly, with colour glowing underneath, then snaps shut. Logo.

## Files
- `stimpy_soundfirst_clean.mp4`: the animatic. It shows the timing, framing and sound to aim for.
- `stimpy_soundfirst_notes.mp4`: the same film with the shoot notes on screen (shot number, what to do). Give this one to whoever is filming.
- `soundfirst_clean_sheet.png`: contact sheet. `.wav`: the animatic soundtrack.

## Kit
- Same as the Edge Zip: macro lens, LED, black cloth
- A quiet room. The sound is the hook.

## Setup
Same setup as the Edge Zip. Record the sound as its own clean take as well.

## Shot list (timed to the animatic)
| Time | Shot | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 s | **Black, sound only** | In the edit: 2–3 s of black over the real zip sound. Add a small "SOUND ON" and a level meter if you like. |
| 2.6 s | **Hard cut on the last teeth** | Cut to the macro just as the pull finishes its run. |
| 3.15 s | **Clack** | The pull hits the stop. Hold still. |
| 4.3 s | **Open it again, slowly** | Pull back slowly: the teeth part and the Stimpie's colour shows through the gap. |
| 6.25 s | **Snap shut** | One fast close. Cut on the click. |
| 7.4 s | **End card** | Add `00_end_cards/stimpy_endcards_logo-black.mp4`. |

## Sound
Most of the film is sound. Use the cleanest recording, and make sure the click lands exactly on the cut.

## Edit
The black opening is the point, so don't trim it shorter than 2 s.

## Caption
"Sound on 🔊"
