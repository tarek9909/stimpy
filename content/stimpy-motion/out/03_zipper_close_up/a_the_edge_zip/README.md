# 03 · A · The Edge Zip

**Shoot brief for the real-footage version of the idea. 9:16, 10 s.**

One slow macro move along the edge of the Stimpie: the pull travels, turns a corner, and the last tooth closes with a click. Nothing is explained, but the corner raises the question: why would a hoodie zipper turn a corner?

## Files
- `stimpy_edgezip_clean.mp4`: the animatic. It shows the timing, framing and sound to aim for.
- `stimpy_edgezip_notes.mp4`: the same film with the shoot notes on screen (shot number, what to do). Give this one to whoever is filming.
- `edgezip_clean_sheet.png`: contact sheet. `.wav`: the animatic soundtrack.

## Kit
- Phone with a macro mode, or a clip-on macro lens (about $10)
- One small LED light
- A black cloth or T-shirt to lay the hoodie on
- A quiet room for the sound

## Setup
Lay the hoodie back-up on the black cloth with the Stimpie half unzipped. Turn the room lights off. Put the LED low and to the side, almost level with the fabric, so the teeth catch the light. The phone is 1–2 cm from the zip.

## Shot list (timed to the animatic)
| Time | Shot | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 s | **Teeth, out of focus** | Start defocused on the open teeth, then pull the focus in (tap to focus in the middle of the take). |
| 0.9 s | **The pull arrives** | Pull slowly and steadily with the other hand out of frame. Keep the coral tab inside the sharp area. |
| 3.2 s | **The corner** | Follow the zip round the corner. Either rotate the phone with it, or keep the phone still and let the corner turn in frame. |
| 5.4 s | **The last tooth** | Slow down for the last teeth. Stop on the tab with the logo sharp. Hold for 1 s without moving. |
| 7.9 s | **End card** | Add `00_end_cards/stimpy_endcards_logo-black.mp4`. |

## Sound
Record the real zip, phone mic about 5 cm away. In the edit, raise it and add a little reverb. No music, or only a very low drone.

## Edit
Shoot 4K 60 fps. You can slow the corner down to 50%.
