You are the lead graphic designer and creative director for **Stimpy**, a streetwear hoodie brand. I'm not giving you a list of designs. **I want you to come up with the ideas yourself.** Read the brand and the print rules below, then surprise me.

---

## 1. The brand

**Stimpy: One hoodie. Multiple identities.**

Stimpy sells a premium heavyweight hoodie with an interchangeable back panel called a **Stimpie**. The Stimpie is a printed cotton panel that zips onto the back of the hoodie in seconds. Customers buy one hoodie and collect many Stimpies, swapping the back design to match their mood, outfit or day. Without a Stimpie, the hoodie is clean and minimal.

- **Personality:** confident, clever, playful, clean. The hoodie stays calm; the Stimpies bring the noise.
- **Audience:** 18–30, style-conscious, lives on Instagram, hates repeating outfits, and prefers clever ideas to big logos.
- **The idea behind the brand:** *two sides, the swap, one person with many identities, constant vs. variable.* You can play with this, but you don't have to in every design.
- **Language:** English only. Any copy should be witty and short, never crude, with no swearing.

**Brand colours.** Work at least one into every design, alongside whatever palette the design needs:
- Coral `#FF7A59` (signature)
- Black `#111111`
- Cream `#FFF4E6`
- Accent options: Electric blue `#2E63FF`, Butter `#FFE45C`, Sky `#5CC8FF`, Lilac `#C9B6FF`

**Brand signature:** a small "split S" mark in the bottom-right corner of every design. It's the letter S cut vertically down the middle: the left half is a solid fill (black, or white on dark designs) and the right half is only an outline in coral, dropped slightly lower than the left half. Keep it small, about 6–7% of the panel height.

---

## 2. Print rules (these are fixed)

- **Format:** portrait, **3:4 ratio**. The final print is 30 × 40 cm, so generate at the highest resolution you can.
- **Full bleed:** the artwork fills the entire canvas with its own background. No transparent background, no hoodie mockup and no t-shirt photo. I need the **flat artwork only**, as it will be printed.
- **Safe zone:** keep all text and key elements away from the outer ~5% of every edge, because seams and the zipper sit there.
- **Text:** if a design uses words, keep them short and spelled correctly.
- **Print quality:** crisp, intentional and sharp. No blurry, smeared, "AI-looking" details, no extra fingers and no garbled text.

**Strictly forbidden:** real brands, logos, trademarks, band names, song lyrics, film titles, celebrities, anime/cartoon characters, sports teams, and copies of known artworks or recognisable artists' signature styles. Everything must be **100% original**.

---

## 3. Your creative brief

Invent a collection of **12 original Stimpie designs**. Everything is your call: the concepts, the art styles, the eras, the subcultures, the characters, the jokes and the copy.

- **Go wide.** Mix moods (loud, minimal and premium, funny, beautiful, strange) and mix techniques (illustration, typography, pattern, collage, print styles from any decade or culture).
- **Be authentic.** Whatever style you choose, research its real visual language and execute it like a top independent print studio would, not like generic clip-art or stock graphics.
- **Make it stop the scroll.** Each design should make someone on Instagram ask "where is that from?"
- **Think collectable.** Stimpies are collected and dropped in limited runs, so at least some ideas should be able to grow into a **series** of 3+ designs.
- **Avoid the obvious.** Generic slogan tees, grumpy-cat memes, "good vibes" type and anything you've seen on every print-on-demand site are out.

---

## 4. How to deliver

1. For each design: a **name**, its **influence** (era, movement or culture), the **idea in one sentence**, and the **image**.
2. For your 3 strongest designs, give me **2 extra variations** each (a different composition or colour treatment).
3. If a design could become a series, describe the next 2 designs in that series in one line each.
4. Finish with your **top 5 for a first drop** and explain in 2–3 sentences why they work together as a set.

**Quality bar:** every piece should look like a limited edition from a cool independent print studio: authentic, sharp, witty and unmistakably Stimpy. When in doubt, choose bold and simple over busy and generic.
