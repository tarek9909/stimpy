# Stimpy videos

Finished videos ready to post are in [`../calendar/posts/`](../calendar/posts/). The projects here make the source videos. Renders stay in each project's `out/` folder, and most aren't in git.

[video-briefs.md](video-briefs.md) has the original creative brief and the shared visual and sound rules.

| Project | What it makes | Calendar posts it feeds | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stimpy-motion/` | Motion graphics: logo, question, split, glimpses, countdowns, zipper and hidden-back films, end cards. It also exports the posters | Most Reels and Stories | [README](stimpy-motion/README.md), [PLAN](stimpy-motion/PLAN.md) |
| `stimpy-video/` | The 50-second "One Hoodie" teaser and the Stimpie card art | Nov 13 film | [README](stimpy-video/README.md) |
| `second_video/` | The 60-second "Multiple Identities" film | Nov 24 film | [README](second_video/README.md) |

The website seed reads card art from `stimpy-video/out/stimpies/`, so changing that folder affects the website.
