# 04 · E · Censored

**Shoot brief for the real-footage version of the idea. 9:16, 10.4 s.**

Bright and sunny, with the camera locked off. Someone walks away down the street with the back pixelated like censored footage, tracked to them. It keeps changing under the mosaic, and twice it glitches: a band of blocks slips for two frames and a sliver of real colour shows.

## Files
- `stimpy_censored_clean.mp4`: the animatic. It shows the timing, framing and sound to aim for.
- `stimpy_censored_notes.mp4`: the same film with the shoot notes on screen (shot number, what to do). Give this one to whoever is filming.
- `censored_clean_sheet.png`: contact sheet. `.wav`: the animatic soundtrack.

## Kit
- A tripod
- A sunny street
- CapCut (mosaic plus motion tracking)
- Several Stimpies to swap between takes

## Setup
Put the tripod at waist height in the middle of the pavement. The subject walks straight away from the camera.

## Shot list (timed to the animatic)
| Time | Shot | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 s | **Walk away, locked off** | The subject walks away, back to us, with a Stimpie on. |
| 1.8 s | **Mosaic in the edit** | In CapCut: Effects → Mosaic on the back, tracked to it, with big blocks. Swap Stimpies between takes and cut on the beat so the design changes under the mosaic. |
| 2.9 s | **Glitch** | Add two or three 2-frame moments where the mosaic slips and a sliver of real colour shows (keyframe the effect off). |
| 3.6 s | **Text** | "The back changes." (add in the edit). |
| 7.6 s | **End card** | Add `00_end_cards/stimpy_endcards_soon.mp4`. |

## Sound
A light pop beat. Add a digital crackle on each glitch.

## Edit
Keep the glitches very short, 2 frames. People will replay to catch them.

## Caption
"Pause it if you can. The back changes. Soon."
