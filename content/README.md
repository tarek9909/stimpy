## Which document wins

1. `calendar/STIMPY_CALENDAR.md` wins on what posts and when, including captions and ads.
2. `identity/stimpy_brand_identity.md` wins on how the brand looks.
3. `strategy/master-plan.md` wins on the product, pricing and market. Its campaign sections came first, so the calendar replaces its dates.

## Where new files go

- **A finished post:** `calendar/posts/YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_type_name.ext`. Use the post's Beirut date and time. A carousel is a folder with the same name.
- **A Reel cover:** `calendar/_covers/`, named after the post with `_cover.jpg` on the end.
- **A replaced post:** move the old version to `calendar/_archive/replaced-originals/`.
- **A new poster:** `posters/`, then add it to the poster table.
- **A new video project:** `videos/`, then add it to the video table.
