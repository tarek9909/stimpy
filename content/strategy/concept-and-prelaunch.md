# Stimpy

**One hoodie. Multiple identities.**

---

## The Concept

Stimpy is a hoodie brand built around one core idea: **interchangeable back designs.**

Most graphic hoodies lock you into a single look. Once the design is printed on the back, that is the only personality the hoodie will ever have. If you want a new look, you buy a new hoodie. Stimpy breaks that cycle.

### The Hoodie

At the heart of Stimpy is a premium, heavyweight hoodie with a clean, minimal design. It is made to be worn on its own, and it looks complete and intentional even with nothing attached to the back. The front stays simple, so the hoodie works with any outfit and any Stimpie.

### The Stimpie

Instead of printing one permanent graphic on the hoodie, the back artwork is a separate piece called a **Stimpie**. A Stimpie is a printed, full-cotton back panel that attaches to the hoodie with a **one-way zipper**. Zipping a Stimpie on or off takes only a few seconds, and each one completely changes the look and personality of the same hoodie.

### How It Works

- You buy one hoodie.
- You collect as many Stimpies as you want.
- You change the back design whenever you want, in seconds.
- Without a Stimpie, the hoodie remains clean and wearable.

### Why It Matters

Stimpy combines three things into one system:

- **Fashion:** a premium hoodie with a strong visual statement on the back.
- **Customization:** the wearer decides what the hoodie says, and can change it to match their mood, outfit, or occasion.
- **Collectability:** Stimpies are designed to be collected. A core collection is always available, while limited drops introduce new designs that will not return once they sell out.

The product is not simply a hoodie with a graphic. It is a system:

> **Hoodie + interchangeable Stimpie = a new look without buying another hoodie.**

## Pre-Launch Plan

### Video Content

#### Motion Graphic Videos

| # | Idea | Description |
|---|------|-------------|
| 1 | **Logo Reveal** | A short animation of the Stimpy logo with the line "One hoodie. Multiple identities. Coming soon." Sets the brand's visual style and gets the name into people's heads. |
| 2 | **The Question** | On-screen text: "How many hoodies do you own just because of the back design?" followed by "What if you only needed one?" Makes people think about the problem before seeing the solution. |
| 5 | **Stimpie Glimpses** | Split-second flashes of the Stimpie designs, or animated pieces of the artwork assembling without ever being fully shown. Builds curiosity about the designs. |
| 7 | **Countdown** | Short daily story countdowns for the final 3 to 5 days before launch, reusing the brand's animations. |
| 9 | **"Which Back Are You?"** | Animated silhouettes of two or three Stimpie designs, paired with story polls. The winning design is revealed first, so followers feel invested before launch. |
| 10 | **The Split Screen** | The same figure, same hoodie, same pose, with the back design changing on every beat of the music. Shows the concept instantly without a single word. |

#### Real Footage Videos

| # | Idea | Description |
|---|------|-------------|
| 3 | **Zipper Close-Up** | Slow, tight shots of the one-way zipper closing, with a satisfying sound and no explanation. Sparks guesses in the comments about what it is for. |
| 4 | **The Hidden Back** | Someone walks away from the camera with the back blurred, covered, or out of frame. Caption: "The back changes. Soon." |
| 6 | **The Reveal** | The full swap: hands unzip one Stimpie and zip on another in a few seconds, then the person turns around. Ends with the launch date. This is the most important video of the campaign. |

### Non-Video Content

**8. Mystery Boxes**
Sealed boxes are sent to 5 to 10 local creators and students with a card that reads "Don't open until [date]." All the unboxings go live on launch day at the same moment, creating a wave of content across Instagram.

**11. Early-Access List**
Followers who DM a specific word get access to the collection 24 hours before everyone else. It rewards the brand's first supporters and shows real interest before launch day.
