- **Pre-launch:** during the four-week teaser period, when the product hasn't been revealed yet.
- **Launch:** from launch day onward, when people can order.
- **Anytime:** works in both phases.

## Part 2: Story Highlight Ideas

Highlight names should stay short, **around 10 to 12 characters or less**, so they don't get cut off under the circle.

**Cover design:** simple white or black icons on circles in the brand colors. Use coral for the most important highlights (ordering and the product), black for brand and community, and the secondary colors for drops.

| # | Highlight name | What goes inside | Cover color |
|---|----------------|------------------|-------------|
| 1 | **How it works** | Short clips showing the swap: unzip one Stimpie, zip on another, done. | Coral |
| 2 | **Order** | Step-by-step ordering: DM or WhatsApp, choose size and Stimpies, pay cash on delivery. | Coral |
| 3 | **Prices** | The packs ($35 with one Stimpie, $45 with two) and extra Stimpie prices. | Coral |
| 4 | **Sizes** | Size chart, how the hoodie fits, and photos of models with their height and size. | Coral |
| 5 | **Delivery** | Delivery areas, timing, fees, and cash on delivery details. | Coral |
| 6 | **Stimpies** | Every available Stimpie design, one per story. | Black |
| 7 | **Drop 01** | Everything from the first drop: teasers, the reveal, and the designs. | Butter |
| 8 | **Next drop** | Hints and teasers for the upcoming drop. Renamed "Drop 02," "Drop 03," and so on as each launches. | Sky |
| 9 | **Reviews** | Screenshots of customer messages and feedback. | Black |
| 10 | **You** | Reposts of customers wearing their hoodies and doing their own swaps. | Black |
| 11 | **Styling** | Outfit ideas: which Stimpie for uni, a night out, or a casual day. | Lilac |
| 12 | **The hoodie** | Close-ups of the fabric, weight, stitching, and details that show quality. | Black |
| 13 | **The zipper** | Close-ups of the one-way zipper and proof that it stays secure. | Black |
| 14 | **Care** | Washing instructions for the hoodie and Stimpies. | Cream |
| 15 | **Exchange** | The exchange policy for sizes and defects, clearly explained. | Coral |
| 16 | **Behind** | Behind the scenes: printing, packing orders, and shooting content. | Black |
| 17 | **Story** | The founder's story and why Stimpy exists. | Black |
| 18 | **Vote** | Past polls, like "Which back are you?", and their results. | Lilac |
| 19 | **FAQ** | Answers to the most common questions, one per story. | Coral |
| 20 | **Collabs** | Artist collaborations and creators wearing Stimpy. | Electric blue |

**Recommended order on the profile:** the circles people need most should come first, since only four or five are visible without scrolling. A strong starting order is:

**How it works → Order → Stimpies → Reviews → Sizes**

Then add the rest behind them as you create the content. At launch, you don't need all 20; start with five to eight and add more as the brand grows. Highlights like Reviews, You, and Collabs only make sense once you have customers and partners to show.
