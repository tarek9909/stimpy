## The business

Stimpy is a new Lebanese streetwear brand: one hoodie with zip-on back panels called Stimpies. Line: "One hoodie. Multiple identities." Instagram: `@stimpy.lb`. Orders come through the website or WhatsApp, paid by cash on delivery.

Documented but not checked on the live store: packs at $35 (hoodie + 1 Stimpie) and $45 (hoodie + 2), costs of $17 and $23, so about $22 margin on the $45 pack before delivery and refusals. Earning back all $450 at that margin needs about 21 delivered orders, which is unlikely in the first month. Treat the month as paid learning.

The biggest risk throughout has been real proof of the zip swap, not how the budget is split. All four reviews reached that conclusion separately.

## Key decisions and why

| Decision | Why |
|---|---|
| No real swap, no ad spend | The buyer's main doubt is whether the zip is real, and illustrations can't answer it |
| Three separate manual campaigns: profile visits, website, WhatsApp | It's the only way to compare the two sales routes |
| Same Arabic Video 1 on website and WhatsApp through Nov 27 | Only the ending, button and destination differ, so the route is what's being compared |
| Website optimizes for Purchase and stays stable | Neither Purchase nor InitiateCheckout will reach about 50 events a week at this budget, and cash on delivery has little checkout friction (an assumption). Changed from InitiateCheckout after audit 1 |
| WhatsApp optimizes for conversations | WhatsApp orders are manual and don't reach Meta |
| Judge on delivered orders, not Meta's numbers | Purchase fires when an order is placed, before refused deliveries |
| Daily budgets | Simple manual pacing |
| Spending limits above planned totals | So the Nov 28 budget move isn't blocked. The account limit of $450 is the cap |
| IREZUMI to BUBBLEGUM as first design pair | Strong contrast, and no obvious third-party reference. SABOTAGE only with written proof |
| No price in ads | Owner's decision, with no evidence either way. Watch drop-off once people see it, and test next month |
| No Black Friday discount | Ads and a discount together can wipe out the margin |
| Retargeting off this month | Audience too small, splits a small budget, and muddies the route comparison |
| Advantage+ audience off this month | Little data on a new Pixel, and a fixed audience keeps routes comparable. This could miss buyers, which is the real cost |
