## Rules for every video

- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio), 1080 x 1920.
- **Safe areas:** keep important text and product shots away from the top 14%, bottom 35% and side 6% of the frame.
- **Audio:** about -14 LUFS, with true peaks no higher than -1 dBTP.
- **Never show:** prices, discounts, 41 x 50 cm, 400 GSM, edition numbers, "100 hoodies", "One slot left" or custom-design claims.
- **Keep it real:** no fake reviews, staged customers or invented product details. Light colour correction only on real footage.
- **Bundles:** don't imply that all designs come with one purchase.
