Stimpy reel shot list S

9:16, 4K 30 fps · locked-off overhead · ~14 s reel · music ~101 BPM (confirm) · 1 beat = 0.594 s

Camera (Pro Video)

  • 1x lens, portrait, UHD 30 (60 for slow-mo)
  • HDR10+ off, LOG off, HEVC on
  • ISO 50–100, shutter 1/50 (1/60 if 60 Hz)
  • WB manual ~5200K, MF focus, EV near 0
  • Auto FPS off, stabilization off
  • Phone level (grid), do not touch between clips

Set

  • Warm mid-tone surface (not white or black)
  • One studio light at the side, room lights off
  • Tape corners where the hoodie sits
  • Props at edges, same place every clip: cassette, receipt, VHS tape, duck
  • You at the bottom edge, plain top, no logos
  • Exposure set per hoodie (black needs light, cream clips)

Premiere

  • Press M on each beat first, then cut to markers
  • Scale 4K clips to 50%, align with Position
  • Zip sound layered quietly under the music
  • Light grade: slight contrast, warm, a touch of saturation
  • Text out of top 14% and bottom 25%

Export

  • H.264, 1080×1920, 30 fps, VBR 1-pass 10–12 Mbps
  • AAC 320 kbps, 48 kHz, under 90 s
  • Add the music in Instagram’s library (not baked in)
  • Transcode or proxy if clips stutter
