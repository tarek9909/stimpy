# Storefront campaign assets

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool, not the CLI. Original catalog artwork and photos were used as references and were not overwritten. These are campaign compositions; catalog cards continue to show the original product assets. Merchant-uploaded homepage images and videos take precedence over the default campaign.
